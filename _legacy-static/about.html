Our story

It started in a friend’s garden

One garden pizza oven, one very good evening, and two friends who couldn’t stop thinking about it. In August 2021, Priten and Vishant took the hint and Pizza Buddies started trading. We’ve been stretching dough in front of crowds ever since.

How it happened

From one oven to every kind of party

The idea was simple and it hasn’t changed: proper Neapolitan pizza — 48-hour dough, ingredients from Italy and local producers, cooked in minutes in Gozney ovens — made right in front of the people eating it.

What has changed is everything around it. Now in our fifth year, we’ve served weddings, mehndi nights, engagements, birthdays, corporate floors and festival fields across Leicestershire and the East Midlands — all of it fully vegetarian, all of it cooked live.

And because it’s just the two of us at the helm, every single booking is confirmed personally. When you enquire, you’re talking to a founder — not a call centre.

The journey

Five seasons of dough and flame

  1. 2021

    A friend fires up a garden pizza oven. The pizzas are good; the idea is better. By August, Pizza Buddies is trading.

  2. Since then

    Weddings, mehndi parties, engagements, birthdays, corporate events and food festivals across the East Midlands — NCASS-registered and Level 2 Food Hygiene certified along the way.

  3. 2026 & beyond

    As we grow, a bespoke fully-fitted pizza trailer is on its way — replacing the gazebo, ready for all weathers and even bigger events. Watch this space.
