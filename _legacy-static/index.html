Why Pizza Buddies

Fully vegetarian. Genuinely Neapolitan.

Most caterers treat vegetarian food as the option in the corner. For us it’s the entire menu — and we’ve spent five seasons proving nobody leaves hungry, or unconvinced.

№ 01

The 48-hour dough

Every base is fermented for two full days before it goes anywhere near a flame — light, blistered, properly Neapolitan. Made with premium ingredients sourced from Italy and local producers.

№ 02

The live show

Our pizzaiolos stretch and top every pizza in front of your guests, then fire it in a Gozney oven in minutes. It’s dinner and entertainment in one 3-metre pitch.

№ 03

Everyone eats

A 100% vegetarian menu with vegan and gluten-free options means one caterer covers the whole guest list — no sad separate plate for anyone.

The live experience

Stretched, fired, served — in front of your guests

  1. Stretch

    Dough that’s been resting for 48 hours is hand-stretched and topped to order, right at the pass.

  2. Fire

    Into the Gozney it goes. Guests watch it blister and rise — every pizza is cooked in minutes.

  3. Serve

    Straight out of the oven and into hands, one after another, until the whole guest list has been fed.
