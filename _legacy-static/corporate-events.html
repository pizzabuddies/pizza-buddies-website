Frequently asked

What events teams ask us

What do you need from our site?

A 3m × 3m space for our gazebo setup and access to a power supply. Car parks, courtyards and outdoor areas all work well — tell us about the site in your enquiry and we’ll flag anything early.

Can you cover vegan and gluten-free colleagues?

Yes. The entire menu is vegetarian, and vegan and gluten-free options are available — let us know numbers when you book and everyone gets fed from the same oven.

Where do you travel?

We’re based in Leicester and serve events across Leicestershire and the East Midlands. Location affects final pricing, which is why we confirm every quote personally.

How does payment work?

A 25% deposit confirms your booking, with the balance due two weeks before the event. Minimum hire starts from £900 depending on location.

Are you certified?

We’re NCASS-registered and Level 2 Food Hygiene certified. Documentation is available for your due diligence on request.
